## AD1 - Supplier as a Domain Concept
Decision:
Supplier was not retained as a separate domain concept
Reason:
Supplier management is not a primary focus of the system and required traceability can be represented through Ingredient Batch

##AD2 - Customer as a Domain Concept
Decision:
Customer was not retained as a separate domain concept
Reason:
Customer management is not major focus of the scenario. Customer related information is represented through Customer Order

##AD3 - Actual Yield
Decision:
Actual yield represented as an attribute of Production Batch rather than as a separate domain concept
